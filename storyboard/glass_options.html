UI direction · Liquid glass

Three ways to make the whole UI liquid glass

Each option shows the same final frame: the dashboard, the Blynk phone and the overlay kit (section tag, label, data pill, route, data tray) over the real device render. Pick one and it applies to every screen in the video.

AClear liquid glass

Almost fully clear glass. Its edges bend what's behind them like a lens, with bright rims and dark text.

  • Pro: the strongest "liquid" look; the device shows through every panel.
  • Con: readability depends on what's behind, so the background has to stay calm.

BFrosted sage glass

Milky, heavily frosted glass tinted light sage, with soft white rims. It's the light, clean flat style you approved, turned into glass.

  • Pro: the calmest and most readable; it fits the light scenes.
  • Con: the least "wow"; the refraction is subtle.

CSmoked green glass CLOSEST TO NEW UI

The dark UI you just approved, turned into tinted, translucent green glass. It keeps the cream text and mono numbers, and adds lens edges and a sage rim light.

  • Pro: the boards already made stay valid; the numbers pop the most.
  • Con: darker overall; the background needs light areas behind the glass.
