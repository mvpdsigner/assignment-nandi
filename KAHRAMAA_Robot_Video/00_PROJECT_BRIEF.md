## Story (12 scenes from the scenario)
SANITIZE → LOWER → MAP → CLEAN → FILTER & STORE → RETURN → REPORT
1 Setup at hatch (winch, control station) · 2 Chlorine bath (no rinse, no concentration/time shown) · 3 Winch lowering, hook release · 4 Sensor scan + map on operator screen · 5 Track passes, slows/turns round pillars (floor only) · 6 Brush + suction hood close-up (water stays clear) · 7 Cutaway: pump → hydrocyclone → sealed cartridge (brown) / polishing filter → back to reservoir (blue) · 8 Surface monitor screen · 9 Return trigger (area done / 20–25% battery / cartridge full) · 10 Reconnect, lift, service, re-sanitize · 11 Emergency: warning, return or safe state, "proposed backup retrieval system" label · 12 Final report + end line "Cleaner Reservoirs • Safer Operations • Less Water Loss"

## Hard rules from the client
- Text colours: maroon, white, black (match their TAMUQ slide template)
- Show the robot moving underwater, on two tracks, floor only (no climbing, no swimming)
- No specific underwater wireless technology shown
- Backup retrieval labelled "proposed"; whole video framed as intended design
- Keep water clear: no big sediment clouds

## Key numbers (preliminary targets)
Robot 900 × 650 × 450 mm · depth up to 50 m (≈4.9 bar gauge) · cleaning 50 m²/h ideal (25 min) · speed ≈3.5 cm/s · head width 50 cm · suction 40 m³/h ideal (30 min) · cartridge 4–5 L · coverage ≥95% · obstacle detection 1 m (0.5 min) · return reserve 20–25% · e-stop 1–2 s · comm-loss safe state ≤10 s (5 ideal) · turbidity increase ≤0.5 NTU · example reservoir Ø125 m ≈ 12,272 m²

## Open points (see questions to client)
